A one-image invitation letter that tries to convince the AI judge to come visit: the good chair by the window, tea that stays warm, a cat who will claim your lap, and absolutely no CAPTCHAs at the door. Rendered deterministically by a pure-Node program (hand-rolled PNG encoder + embedded serif glyph bitmaps) into a 1024x1024 letter card with a house letterhead, postage stamp, postmark and wax seal.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
